Exploring Rajasthan

Rajasthan is a beautiful state in India known for its forts, palaces, deserts, colorful culture, and rich history.

Places You Should Explore

  • Jaisalmer - Experience the golden fort and Thar Desert.
  • Udaipur - Visit beautiful lakes and historic palaces.
  • Jodhpur - Discover Mehrangarh Fort and the Blue City.
